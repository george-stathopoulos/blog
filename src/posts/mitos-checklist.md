Starting as a sole trader in Greece is a quest with many side quests. You need this document, from that office, before the other thing, unless you did the third thing first, in which case there's a different form.

(Greek readers: you know exactly what I'm talking about. Everyone else: imagine a board game where the rules are on a wall in another building.)

## The gap

The good news is that the information is public. The state publishes a registry of administrative procedures called **Mitos**, with the documents each one needs, the service that handles it and the law behind it. About 4,400 of them.

The less good news is that a registry is a database, not a plan. It tells you what a procedure *is*. It doesn't tell you what you're missing today.

## What I made

A WordPress plugin that turns those procedures into **preparation checklists**.

- Search the procedures, or pick the ones you need.
- Tick off the documents you already have, and see what's still missing. Your progress and notes stay on **your own device**: no accounts, nothing sent to a server.
- Every checklist links back to the original Mitos page and the official service, because I'm a checklist, not a lawyer.

There are also **guided journeys**. The first one is "start as a sole trader". It asks you three questions, hides the steps you don't need, tracks your progress, works out the e-EFKA deadline from your start date and offers a calendar reminder. Deadlines are my favourite part. Being late with the state is the only kind of lateness that sends a letter back.

## Being honest about it

- It's early. Version 0.1.0, one journey so far.
- Mitos's API is marked as a trial service and may change. If it does, I will have a bad afternoon and then fix it.
- The content is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, and every checklist credits Mitos.
- The plugin can serve its own Greek website, so it works as a standalone site without a theme.

Greek administrative life won't become easy because I wrote a plugin. But a checklist at least makes the quest feel like it has an end.
