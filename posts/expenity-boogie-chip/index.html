Boogie and Chip: two friends for one money app (and they do not agree on anything)

21 Oct 2026 · 2 min read · #webapp #finance #privacy #greek

eFriend now comes in two personalities. Boogie is a vintage cassette with a glowing tube. Chip is a hyped wireless robot. Same money answers, very different voices, and a story about a cable.

I gave my money app a friend. Then I gave him a flatmate.

When you open Expenity for the first time, you pick who lives in it. A split screen, two characters, one slider. Slide left or right, tap the one you like. That's it. 🎚️

📼 Boogie: the old soul

  • 📼 A vintage cassette with a glowing Nixie tube for a face.
  • 😏 Warm, a little dry, nostalgic about the eighties and analog sound.
  • ✏️ Loves a good plan and a better pencil. He writes everything on an old tape recorder.
  • 💡 The tube glow is yours to set: Low, Normal or High, in amber, blue or fluorescent green.
  • 🎛️ The settings live on a brass control plate, with a bat-handle toggle and little lamp buttons. Yes, it clicks.

🤖 Chip: the fast one

  • 🤖 A young wireless robot. Fast, hyped, playful.
  • 🎉 Full of emoji and bad battery jokes. ("I'm at 12% and still 100% excited.")
  • 💻 Writes on a computer and burns CDs. Obviously.
  • 😎 Insists he is the smarter one.

🔌 How they met

Chip tried to connect to Bluetooth. Boogie offered him a cable and said:

"We don't do invisible friendships."

Chip still says he is the smarter one. Boogie points out that Chip has never once fixed anything with a pencil. They have been arguing happily ever since. ❤️

💶 The money answers are the same

This is the important bit. Pick either one and you get the same three answers, worked out the same way, on your device:

  • ✅ Can you afford it?
  • 📅 What is the best day to buy?
  • 🛡️ Is it safe to spend?

Only the words change. One says it with a raised eyebrow and a warm tape hiss. The other says it with three emoji and a lightning bolt. Same numbers, zero disagreement on the maths.

📖 The eFriend Blog

Both keep a little blog inside the app, the eFriend Blog. It retells your money story, your paydays, your bills, your close calls, in the voice of the friend you chose. Same facts, two storytellers. Pick the one you'd want at the kitchen table.

Each one also has their own jokes. I'm not telling you which ones are better. (Boogie's. Fine, Chip, yours too.)

Choose your friend

  • 🎮 Meet them in the eFriend page
  • 💬 Chat with the first eFriend, live, right here on the blog
  • 🌐 Look around the Expenity website
  • 📱 Open Expenity

Free, open source, English and Greek, nothing to download. Slide the slider. You can always change your mind. They will pretend not to be hurt. 📼🤖
